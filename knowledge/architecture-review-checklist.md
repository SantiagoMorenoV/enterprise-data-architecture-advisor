# Enterprise Architecture Review Checklist

## Business Alignment

- Is a business sponsor identified?
- Are business capabilities documented?
- Are business outcomes measurable?

## Governance

- Is a Data Owner identified?
- Is a Data Steward identified?
- Is lineage documented?
- Is metadata managed?

## Security

- Is RBAC implemented?
- Is encryption defined?
- Is auditing required?

## Operations

- Is monitoring defined?
- Is SLA defined?
